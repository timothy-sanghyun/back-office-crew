# Presentation script (easy English)

1. **Back-Office Crew** — Hello, I am Timothy. My project is Back-Office Crew. It is a team of AI agents that controls food cost for small restaurants.
2. **Small restaurant owners** — Our customer is a small restaurant owner. One person does three jobs: purchasing, cost control, and kitchen planning.
3. **Food cost changes every week** — But nobody monitors it. Price increases are found late. Calculation is manual. Decisions are disconnected.
4. **7 functions → 7 agents** — We found seven functions. We made one agent for each function, like job roles in a company.
5. **Agents decide. The system calculates.** — Agents make decisions, but all numbers come from calculation tools. Nothing is executed without the owner's approval.
6. **One price change → one approval** — Detect, analyze, then purchasing and menu work in parallel. The owner approves once.
7. **Numbers come from formulas** — Food cost is recipe cost divided by menu price. The supplier is chosen by the lowest landed cost per usable kilogram.
8. **Avocado price went up 50%** — The market price is only 43 to 44 dollars. 1,685 dollars of profit per month is at risk.
9. **Demo** — (play the video) The agents check the price, get quotes, fix the menu, and the owner approves.
10. **$717 per month recovered** — We split the order between two suppliers. The critic blocked one idea: we do not cut the signature guacamole.
11. **Two screens** — The owner sees numbers and one approval button. The kitchen sees each cook's tasks.
12. **How we built it** — ZooWork runs the seven agents. Band is the shared chat room. Tavily searches market prices. Slack sends the approval request.
13. **Next steps** — Real POS data, labor cost, and waste. The goal is the full P&L.
14. **Close** — One price change. One approval. 717 dollars per month back. Thank you.
